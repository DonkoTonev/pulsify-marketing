# Algorithm playbook (living document)
Updated by the weekly research and the Monday review. Our own Postiz analytics beat any claim from the web.

## TikTok (seed, Nov 2026)
- Strongest signals: watch time, completion rate, replays, shares. Then saves, comments, follows. Likes are weaker.
- The first 1-2 s decide most of it: hook in text and voice at once, no logo or intro first.
- Shorter is easier to finish: start around 20 s, go longer only if our data shows completion holds.
- Endings that loop back to the start encourage replays.
- Give people a reason to share or save: useful numbers, lists, "прати на някой, който...".
- Ask one simple question to start comments ("Колко мислите, че е?").
- TikTok works like a search engine: say the keyword in the voiceover, show it on screen and put it in the caption (e.g. "калории баница").
- New videos are tested on a small group first and spread over 24 h or more; do not judge a post in its first hours.
- Staying in one niche (Bulgarian food and calories) helps the algorithm find the right audience.

## Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts
- Same core: retention and shares. Never upload a video with another platform's watermark.
