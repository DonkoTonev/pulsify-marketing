# Hooks log & patterns

Every produced video's hook + opening frame + quality scores go here, plus the short list of
best-performing hook *patterns* to reuse. Never repeat an exact hook. Our own results rank patterns;
web claims are secondary.

## Best patterns so far (reuse the shape, not the words)
1. **Belief-challenge / claim-lead** — tell the viewer their intuition is wrong, up front.
   - "мислиш шопската е лека?" -> 588 views day 1 (our best so far). Day-1 winner vs the soft opener.
   - Shape: "мислиш [храна] е [здравословна/лека]?" or "[храна] има повече калории от [храна]".
2. **Two-food face-off** — "Кое има повече калории: X или Y?" with the two foods on screen from
   frame 0. Works when the answer is counter-intuitive. (Monday app format.)
3. **Surprising single number + relatable comparison** — reveal a big kcal number, then translate it
   ("колкото 2 дюнера"). Drives the share.

## Avoid (confirmed by our data + 10.1 kill list)
- Soft "Колко калории има в [X]?" as the *opening line* — banitsa used it, 175 views day 1 (< the
  claim-lead shopska). Fine as a story beat, not as frame-0 hook.
- Naming the app in the hook; feature lists; "Знаете ли, че..."; plain facts without tension.
